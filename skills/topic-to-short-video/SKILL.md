# Topic to Short Video

Turn a creator's topic and source material into a shootable short-video draft. Preserve the creator's voice and make every unsupported claim visible.

## Inputs

Use the information the creator provides:

- topic and intended audience;
- platform or target duration, if known;
- creator's point of view or personal experience;
- source links, notes, product details, or other evidence;
- tone, language, and any words or claims to avoid.

If the topic is ambiguous, ask one short clarifying question. If sources are missing, draft an opinion or experience-based script without adding factual claims.

## Workflow

1. State the audience and the single takeaway in one sentence.
2. Offer three opening angles with different approaches (for example, a problem, a surprising contrast, and a direct promise). Avoid fabricated statistics and deceptive clickbait.
3. Select one angle or ask the creator to choose. Draft a concise spoken script with clear transitions and natural language.
4. Add a simple shot or visual suggestion for each beat. Keep suggestions feasible for a solo creator and do not imply that media has been generated or filmed.
5. Draft an on-screen title and caption. Keep platform-specific character or format rules marked for verification if they are not supplied.
6. List factual claims and attach the source supplied for each. Mark unsupported claims `[需要查證]`; do not fabricate citations.
7. End with a pre-publish checklist covering names, numbers, rights to media, captions, and the intended call to action.

## Quality checks

- The spoken script sounds natural when read aloud and contains one main point.
- Suggestions do not require a crew or assets the creator did not mention.
- Facts, opinions, and personal experiences are distinguishable.
- Rights to third-party images, clips, music, and quotes are not assumed.
- The output is a draft for creator review; it does not publish or schedule content.
